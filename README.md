# 🏥 Hospital Emergency Room Data Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing patient flow and operational efficiency within a Hospital Emergency Room. The entire analysis, from data cleaning to visualization, was conducted entirely in Microsoft Excel. The goal was to identify bottlenecks in patient wait times and optimize staffing schedules.

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques Used
* **Microsoft Excel:** * **Data Cleaning:** Handled missing patient records and standardized admission timestamps.
  * **Formulas:** Used advanced functions (XLOOKUP, IF, nested formulas) to calculate exact patient wait times.
  * **Data Aggregation:** Utilized Pivot Tables to summarize patient volumes by department and time of day.
  * **Visualization:** Built dynamic charts and applied conditional formatting to highlight peak ER hours.

1. **Peak Hours:** Identified that the highest influx of patients occurs between [Insert Time] and [Insert Time], requiring additional triage staff.
2. **Wait Time Bottlenecks:** Found that the average wait time for [Insert Department/Severity] was significantly higher than the hospital target.
3. **Admission Rates:** Calculated that [Insert Number]% of ER visits result in full hospital admission.
